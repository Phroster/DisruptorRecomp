DISRUPTOR RECOMPILED
Play the PlayStation classic Disruptor as a native Windows game.

This is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project. It is not affiliated with
the game's owners.

WHAT YOU NEED
- A 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC with an OpenGL 3.3 graphics card.
- Your own Disruptor disc image, USA version (.bin or .iso).
  If you have a .cue and a .bin file, pick the .bin.
- About 1 GB of free space while installing.

HOW IT WORKS
This download contains no part of the game. Setup reads the game's program
from your disc image, turns it into a Windows program on your PC and checks
every step. That takes about a minute. Your original disc image is never
changed; Setup keeps its own copy in the game folder.

PLAYING
Open "Disruptor Recompiled" from the Start menu and click PLAY DISRUPTOR.
If the game still needs to be built, the button says BUILD THE GAME.

Default controls:
  W / S                 move forward / back
  A / D                 strafe
  Mouse                 turn
  Left click / L-Ctrl   fire, confirm
  Right click / F       psionic power
  Mouse wheel           change weapon (hold R and scroll for psionic powers)
  Q / R                 hold for the weapon / psionic list
  Enter / Esc           pause
In menus, W / S or the arrow keys move and Left Ctrl confirms.
Controllers work too.

Hotkeys: F7 savestates, Alt+Enter fullscreen, Tab fast-forward (F9 keeps it
on), F6 scanlines, F10 FPS counter, keypad + / - volume.

Change settings and keys in the launcher (Settings and Controls).

YOUR FILES
Saves: %LOCALAPPDATA%\DisruptorRecompiled\saves
Logs:  %LOCALAPPDATA%\DisruptorRecompiled\logs
The launcher's "Saves folder" and "Logs folder" buttons open them.

UPDATING AND UNINSTALLING
To update, close the game and run the new Setup. It remembers your disc and
keeps your settings and saves.
To uninstall, use Windows Settings > Apps. Your saves and settings stay.

PROBLEMS?
Report them at https://github.com/Phroster/DisruptorRecomp/issues and attach
the logs from the launcher's "Logs folder".
